## Admission boundary

`services/love/src/admission.ts` resolves the signed-in account through the existing Platform Accounts service and caller-scoped RestClient. Only an active Employee bound to that account becomes the LiveKit identity; browser `_id` and name cannot choose another person. Meeting and room reads use the caller token, preserving Platform space ACLs. Missing/finished meetings, DND and read-only guests fail closed. Public shared rooms retain direct self-join; personal offices require their existing guest/Knock rule.

Invitations remain `love.class.UserMeetingInvite` documents in existing PersonSpaces. `/inviteMeeting` creates a signed pending request; the retained trigger copies its receipt to the recipient's response. `/respondToMeetingInvite` binds the authenticated recipient, request, workspace, room and meeting before issuing a signed acceptance. A restricted-room invitation must involve the room host; changing a client-writable status to `accepted` is insufficient. Request expiry is 30 seconds; accepted receipts last at most eight hours and cannot admit to a finished/deleted/inaccessible meeting. Removing the request revokes future token issuance. Already-issued LiveKit tokens retain their existing 10-minute life; immediate connected-participant eviction remains a separate follow-up.

Frontend acceptance now precedes token admission. The existing request persists for reconnect checks, and its optional `joined` flag prevents repeated automatic invitation joins. A legitimate shared-room host may start their own Knock meeting. DND remains blocked for everyone, matching the existing frontend. Responses are serialized per invitation within the single configured Love replica; accept/decline races resolve in order, and a failed recipient-record update can recover from its already accepted request. Full and compact Office still observe the same retained room/meeting/presence documents. Existing presence is moved only after token admission and successful LiveKit connection. Webhook/polling presence remains the upstream authority for media connection; there is no new Chief room database.

The model only adds optional `admission` and `joined` fields to UserMeetingInvite. Existing records remain valid. Legacy unsigned invitations cannot authorize restricted-room entry; send a new invitation. There is no data reset or automatic conversion of earlier Chief Office data.
