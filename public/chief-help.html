Getting started with Chief

Your personal team of AI agents, on your computer.

  1. Open Settings and connect a supported model provider under AI. Use your own account or API credentials. Provider usage and billing are separate from Chief.
  2. Create an agent, choose its model, and describe what you want it to do.
  3. Start a conversation. Review requested permissions before allowing access to files, tools, or your computer.

Your work stays with you

Chief stores its local workspace separately from other applications. In Advanced settings, use Backups to export an encrypted recovery copy. Keep the backup password safe.

Tasks and routines

Keep Chief running and your computer awake for local tasks and scheduled routines. A disconnected provider or sleeping computer can interrupt work. Check the conversation before retrying an interrupted action.

Updates and support

This release uses manual updates. Obtain replacement installers from the person who supplied Chief. Preserve your workspace and make a backup before upgrading. When reporting a problem, include the version from About Chief and the steps that reproduce it; do not include passwords or API keys.

Separate workspace product

Chief Exec Workspace is a separate product under development. It is not required to use your personal Chief agents.
